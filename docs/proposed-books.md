# Proposed books

This is a proposal for 33 books to add to the catalog, so that every theme in [`theme-vocabulary.md`](theme-vocabulary.md) has at least 2 books. **Nothing here is in `data/books.csv` yet.** Once a book is approved, tag it in full using [`tagging-guide.md`](tagging-guide.md).

Before this proposal, 21 of the 32 themes had fewer than 2 books (`freedom`, `purpose`, `hope` and `leadership` had none). To check coverage with these books included:

```bash
python3 scripts/check_coverage.py --with-proposed
```

## Things to check before adding

- **Page counts:** lengths are approximate and vary by edition. I'm least sure of *Stay Hungry Stay Foolish* and *Wings of Fire*.
- ***Prejudice* is heavy:** 9 proposed books carry `prejudice`. If heavy picks crowd out others, drop it from a few books where it is secondary (*Wonder*, *Pride and Prejudice*).
- **Repeat authors:** Divakaruni and Backman each appear twice in the catalog. This is deliberate: readers who like one book often want another by the same author.
- **Content notes:** content notes and full tags are not in this proposal yet. They will be needed for *A Fine Balance*, *Train to Pakistan*, *The Kite Runner*, *The God of Small Things* and *I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings*, which all deal with violence or abuse.
