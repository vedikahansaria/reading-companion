# Rules for this repo

- **Keep the repo tidy.** Only `data/`, `docs/` and `scripts/` hold project files, plus `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` at the root. Don't leave draft, scratch or temporary files behind.
- **Use only themes from `docs/theme-vocabulary.md`** in `themes` and `leads_to` in `data/books.csv`, and in both theme columns of `data/theme_journeys.csv`. To add a theme, add it to the vocabulary first.
- **Log product decisions in `docs/decisions.md`**, with the date, the decision and why.
- **Write in plain language.** Use short sentences and everyday words. No em dashes.
